What is a Free Custom Growth Plan?

It is a complimentary review of your business across five areas: visibility, trust, acquisition, conversion and efficiency. We look at how customers find you, what happens after they contact you and where time or money is being lost, then give you a clear, prioritized list of opportunities.

Is the Growth Plan really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation. You keep the plan whether you work with us or not. If you want help implementing it, we will send you a written proposal first.

Why do you only take 7 Growth Plans per month?

Every plan is built by hand by our founders, not generated by a template. Limiting the number each month is how we keep each diagnosis specific to the business in front of us.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on what your business actually needs, which is why we diagnose before we prescribe. After your Growth Plan you receive a written proposal with clear scope and pricing before any work begins.

Do I have to hire you for everything?

No. Some clients only need a website or a follow-up system. Others need several pieces working together. We recommend only what will make the biggest impact for your business.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Small and growing businesses, especially local and owner-operated service companies such as home services, tree services, landscaping, cleaning, remodeling, painting, roofing, concrete, detailing and contractors. We also work with hospitality and other businesses that need better systems.

Do you only work with businesses in Charlotte?

We are based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and work with businesses across the United States and Latin America. Most of our work can be done remotely.

Can you work with me in Spanish or Portuguese?

Yes. Our team works in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and we can build bilingual websites and marketing for your customers.

How is NOVA different from a marketing agency?

Most agencies sell one piece, such as ads, a website or social media. NOVA looks at the whole system behind growth, from visibility and marketing to sales follow-up, technology and automation, and fixes the part that is actually holding you back.

What does AI and automation mean for a small business?

It means less manual work: automatic lead follow-up, appointment reminders, review requests, connected tools and dashboards. For an international resort in Orlando we built a housekeeper assignment system that reduced a four-hour daily process to five minutes.

How long does it take to see results?

Some improvements, like faster lead follow-up or a better Google Business Profile, can show impact within weeks. Others, like SEO, build over months. Your Growth Plan will set realistic expectations for each recommendation.
